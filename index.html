<h2>ABOUT BÁHLIAT</h2>


<p>

Báhliat is a city located on Crimson Courtant Island.

The city is considered to be one of the more isolated

settlements on the island.

</p>


<p>

Information regarding the city is limited and some official

records concerning Báhliat are unavailable or incomplete.

</p>


<p>

The location is generally considered to be

<b>unsafe for unauthorized visitors.</b>

</p>

<h2>SAFETY INFORMATION</h2>


<p class="warning">

WARNING: BÁHLIAT IS NOT CONSIDERED A SAFE LOCATION.

</p>


<p>

Several incidents have been reported in and around the city.

These include missing persons, unexplained sounds, unusual

radio transmissions and unidentified lights during nighttime.

</p>


<p>

Some areas surrounding Báhliat are restricted.

Unauthorized entry into these areas is strongly discouraged.

</p>



<h2>INVESTIGATIONS</h2>


<p>

Special agents have reportedly been sent to Báhliat to

investigate incidents occurring within the city and the

surrounding areas.

</p>


<p>

Reported investigation subjects include:

</p>


<ul>


    <li>Missing persons</li>


    <li>Unidentified radio transmissions</li>


    <li>Abandoned buildings</li>


    <li>Restricted areas</li>


    <li>Underground structures</li>


    <li>Unusual activity during nighttime</li>


</ul>

<h2>INCIDENTS</h2>


<div class="box">


    <p>

        <b>INCIDENT 01</b>

    </p>


    <p>

        Three people reportedly disappeared while travelling

        through the western outskirts of Báhliat.

    </p>


    <p>

        No confirmed explanation has been published.

    </p>


</div>



<div class="box">


    <p>

        <b>INCIDENT 02</b>

    </p>


    <p>

        Several residents reported hearing footsteps outside

        their homes during the early hours of the morning.

    </p>


    <p>

        No person was found in the area.

    </p>


</div>



<div class="box">


    <p>

        <b>INCIDENT 03</b>

    </p>


    <p>

        An investigation team reported that their radios

        stopped functioning at approximately 03:17.

    </p>


    <p>

        Communication was restored several hours later.

    </p>


</div>



<h2>IMPORTANT INFORMATION</h2>


<p class="warning">

DO NOT ENTER RESTRICTED AREAS.

</p>


<p>

Do not enter abandoned buildings without authorization.

Do not travel alone through Veyra Woods.

Avoid unexplained lights or sounds during nighttime.

</p>


<p>

If approached by an unknown individual claiming to be

an agent or investigator, verification of identification

is recommended.
